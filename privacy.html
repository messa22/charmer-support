Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 25, 2026

Charmer is an AI texting assistant. This policy explains how we handle information when you use the app.

Information We Process

Charmer may process profile preferences you enter in the app, text prompts, and screenshots you choose to upload so the app can generate reply ideas. Generated messages and preferences may be stored locally on your device.

How We Use Information

We use your inputs to provide AI-generated replies, openers, bios, and chat analysis. Screenshots and prompts are sent to our backend service for processing and are used only to generate the requested result.

Payments

Purchases and subscriptions are processed by Apple's App Store. We do not receive or store your payment card details. Subscription status may be managed through RevenueCat using anonymous purchase identifiers.

Third-Party Services

Charmer may use backend, AI, analytics, and subscription infrastructure providers to operate the app. These services process data only as needed to provide app functionality.

Contact

For privacy questions or deletion requests, contact charmer.app.nl@gmail.com.
