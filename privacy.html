Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 14, 2026

Zexy is an AI texting assistant. This policy explains how we handle information when you use the app.

Information We Process

Zexy may process profile preferences you enter in the app, text prompts, selected tone and style settings, usage counters, and screenshots you choose to upload so the app can generate reply ideas. Screenshots and prompts may contain personal information visible in a chat, profile, or story. Generated messages and preferences may be stored locally on your device.

How We Use Information

We use your inputs to provide AI-generated replies, openers, bios, and chat analysis. After you give permission in the app, screenshots, prompts, and generation settings are sent to Zexy's secure Supabase backend and Anthropic's Claude AI service. This data is used only to generate the requested result and is not stored by Zexy after the response is returned.

Payments

Purchases and subscriptions are processed by Apple's App Store. We do not receive or store your payment card details. Subscription status may be managed through RevenueCat using anonymous purchase identifiers.

Third-Party Services

Zexy uses Supabase for backend processing, Anthropic Claude for AI generation, and RevenueCat for subscription status management. These providers process data only as needed to provide app functionality and are expected to protect the data they process under their own security and privacy commitments.

Contact

For privacy questions or deletion requests, contact charmer.support@gmail.com.
